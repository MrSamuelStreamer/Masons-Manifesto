Mason's Manifesto by SealOfApproval is a collection of lightweight buildings to add flare and certified build-base approval to your Rimworld colonies.

- 3 wall-attached window variants 
- 4 door variants, including wooden and stone castle gates
- flying buttress for medieval decor
- 6 arches for decorating passage ways
- VE Props integrations and category for new additions
